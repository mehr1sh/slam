# Trajectory error diagnostics: report

**Scope.** This is a diagnostic investigation only. The PnP/ICP algorithms, their
parameters, the correspondence rules, the dataset, the accumulation and the
Blender scene were **not changed**. Every number below comes from the files in
this directory, regenerated by:

```bash
pixi run build
pixi run -e results diagnostics   # scripts/run_diagnostics.py
blender -b visualization/scenes/bunny_slam_demo.blend \
    --python scripts/check_blender_consistency.py -- diagnostics/blender_consistency.csv
```

**How the data was produced.**
- `slam_trajectory_test` ran with the export-only flags
  `--export-dir … --export-pair all` on a copy of the dataset. Its trajectory
  outputs were byte-identical to the committed files.
- The standalone `trajectory_diagnostics_test` then repeated the **same solver
  calls with the same arguments** on every pair. It reproduced every exported
  count and relative error exactly, and the trajectory files to their
  6-decimal rounding (7.8e-7 m).
- Diagnostic re-solves on correspondence subsets (marked *diagnostic*) are
  measurements of sensitivity, not proposed fixes. Some of them select
  correspondences **using ground truth**, which no real system could do.

---

## 1. Executive summary

**NO BUG FOUND.** The accumulation, the transform conventions, the ICP
inversion and the error metrics are verified correct:
- The GT replay is exact (5e-16 m), and deliberately wrong formulas fail at
  once.
- Both solvers recover ground truth to 1e-5° when given perfect
  correspondences.
- Blender matches the CSV to 3e-8 m.

The trajectory error comes from the **correspondences fed to correct solvers**:

1. **Systematic under-estimation of every 10° step** (PnP 9.13° mean, ICP 8.55°).
   - Matched ORB features move **less** in the image than the surface points
     they are assumed to be: the observed flow is a median 0.945 (mean 0.918)
     of the flow ground truth predicts.
   - The lag is strongest at the bunny's silhouette and self-occlusion edges
     (ratio 0.72–0.89). These are view-dependent contour features, not fixed
     3D points, and 66% of PnP correspondences lie within 4 px of the
     silhouette.
   - Per pair, this lag correlates with PnP's rotation ratio at r = +0.70.
   - The deficits all have the same sign, so they add up: the summed deficit
     is −30.4° for PnP (final error 32.1°) and −50.8° for ICP (final 64.9°).
2. **ICP's large steps (24→25, 13→14, 28→29)** come from gross 3D mismatches:
   - 12–16 per pair with a residual above 2 cm at the true motion, clustered
     on the ear tip and silhouette.
   - The book ICP has no outlier rejection. Its unweighted least-squares fit
     (SVD; g2o gives the same answer) ends with a **lower** RMS residual than
     the true motion in 35/35 pairs, so its cost minimum is not the truth.
   - In 24→25 the rotation magnitude is nearly right (9.18°) but the axis is
     87° off, which gives a 13.25° error.

Classification:
- correspondence quality: **primary**
- algorithmic limitation (no robust ICP): **primary for the ICP spikes**
- depth: minor
- transform: **excluded**
- bug: **none found**

## 2. GT replay (highest-priority test)

The GT relative motions `T_gt[i+1]⁻¹·T_gt[i]` were fed through the pipeline's
exact accumulation `T_wc[i+1] = T_wc[i]·T_rel⁻¹` (`gt_replay.csv`):

| | Max over 36 frames |
|---|---|
| translation error | **5.2e-16 m** |
| rotation error | **0.0°** |

**PASS**, so the investigation continued. The negative controls show the test
is sensitive (`gt_replay_controls.csv`):

| Control | Error at frame 1 | Max over frames |
|---|---|---|
| no inverse | 0.174 m / 20.0° | 1.00 m / 180° |
| wrong multiplication side | 0.269 m / 19.85° | 1.67 m / 178.3° |

## 3. Transform convention

See `transform_convention.md`. All four conventions (stored T_wc, PnP
T_{j←i}, ICP native T_{i←j} plus inversion, accumulation) are confirmed by
four independent tests:
- the GT replay
- perfect-correspondence PnP (5.1e-6°)
- perfect-correspondence ICP after the pipeline's own inversion (5.4e-6°)
- Python re-accumulation

The half-pixel principal-point mismatch between the rasterizer and OpenCV is
real but negligible: PnP's mean rotation goes from 9.132° to 9.138°.

## 6. Correspondence quality (`correspondence_metrics.csv`, `flow_bias_by_feature_location.csv`)

**Counts per pair (mean, min–max):**

| Quantity | Mean | Range |
|---|---|---|
| filtered matches | 191.7 | 127–253 |
| PnP correspondences | 142.5 | 72–206 |
| PnP inliers | 132 | 67–197 |
| inlier ratio | 0.924 | 0.863–0.978 |
| ICP correspondences | 128.5 | 62–189 |

- The fewest correspondences are at 31→32 (PnP 72, ICP 62), then 30→31 and
  28→29.
- About 26% of filtered matches have no depth (`depth_metrics.csv`).

**Counts do not explain the errors** (`relative_error_vs_correspondence_count.png`):

| Correlation with relative rotation error | r |
|---|---|
| PnP correspondence count | −0.24 |
| ICP correspondence count | −0.32 |
| PnP inlier ratio | −0.08 |

The worst PnP pairs have ordinary counts (102–155).

**What does explain them is where the features are.**
- 66.5% of PnP correspondences lie within 4 px of the bunny's silhouette
  (black background, small object).
- Measured against the GT-predicted image position of the same 3D point, the
  inlier matches **lag the true motion**:

| Feature location (PnP inliers) | n | Median observed/GT flow |
|---|---|---|
| interior ≥ 5 px, smooth depth | 1316 | **0.978** |
| interior, at a depth edge (> 5 mm) | 129 | 0.934 |
| 2–4 px from silhouette, smooth | 697 | 0.896 |
| 2–4 px from silhouette, depth edge | 185 | **0.790** |
| on silhouette (≤ 1 px), smooth | 955 | 0.954 |
| on silhouette, depth edge | 378 | 0.873 |

- Contour and occlusion features are not fixed surface points. As the camera
  orbits, the outline slides over the surface, so the matched pixel moves
  less than the 3D point back-projected from frame i.
- The per-pair median flow ratio correlates with PnP's estimated/GT rotation
  at **r = +0.70** (ICP: +0.10, because ICP is dominated by gross outliers;
  see §8).
- *Diagnostic:* re-solving PnP on only the correspondences that agree with
  GT to < 1 px (mean 42 of 142) gives 9.944° (error 0.28°). The solver is
  accurate when the correspondences are.
- *Diagnostic:* removing only features near the silhouette (≥ 4 px in both
  frames, mean 54 left) is **not** enough: 9.55°, error 1.74°. Interior
  self-occlusion edges also lag, and fewer points cost precision.

All correspondences: median 1.72 px. The max (≈ 47–173 px) is gross mismatches,
all rejected by RANSAC.

**Anomalies:**
- On noise-free renders, ~1.5 px median inlier reprojection error is large. It
  is the feature lag of §6, not image noise: the inliers' error at the **GT**
  motion is 1.54 px median, about the same as at the estimated pose.
- The highest inlier means are at 23→24 (2.53 px), 24→25 (2.52 px) and
  11→12 (2.35 px).
- In 24→25, ear-tip features with 8–10 px GT error are still inliers under the
  8 px threshold, because they pull the estimated pose towards themselves
  (`pnp_pair_24_25.png`, blue segments).

## 8. ICP residuals (`icp_residuals.csv`, `icp_residual_before_after.png`)

ICP **does reduce the 3D residuals**. Over pairs, the residual
‖p_i − (R p_j + t)‖ goes:

| Statistic | Before (identity) | After SVD + g2o |
|---|---|---|
| median | 8.4 mm | 3.9 mm |
| mean | 11.9 mm | 8.1 mm |

Both drop in 34/35 pairs.

**The catch:**
- At the **true** motion, the median residual is 2.3 mm, *lower* than ICP's
  3.9 mm.
- The RMS at the true motion (19.8 mm) is *higher* than ICP's (19.3 mm) in
  **35/35 pairs**.
- So the least-squares optimum is not the truth. ~5.5% of ICP pairs (249/4499)
  are gross mismatches with > 2 cm residual at the true motion; the largest
  are 137 mm in 13→14 and 79 mm in 24→25.
- The squared cost lets them pull the fit away from the many good pairs.
- The per-pair count of gross mismatches correlates with ICP's rotation error
  at r = +0.67.
- *Diagnostic:* re-solving on pairs with < 5 mm residual at GT gives a mean
  error of 0.71° (vs 2.81°).

## 9. SVD vs g2o (`svd_vs_g2o.csv`)

g2o (10 LM iterations, initialised at identity, not at the SVD result)
converges to the SVD solution in every pair:

| Max difference over 35 pairs | Value |
|---|---|
| rotation | 8.9e-5° |
| translation | 7.8e-7 m |

Initialising g2o at identity is therefore **not** a cause. The SVD reflection
branch (det < 0) was never needed: the perfect-correspondence ICP is exact,
and earlier checks found 0/35.

## 10. Depth quality (`depth_metrics.csv`)

**Depth consistency:** the measured depth at the frame-j keypoint vs the
depth GT predicts for the frame-i point (ICP pairs):

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| median | 1.1 mm |
| p90 | 6.4 mm |
| max per pair | 18–104 mm |
| share inconsistent by > 1 cm | 7.0% |

- About 21% of ICP pairs sit at a depth edge (> 5 mm jump within 3×3).
- The large inconsistencies occur at silhouettes and depth edges. There, the
  depth read at the truncated keypoint pixel can come from a different
  surface than the matched feature. This feeds the ICP gross outliers of §8.
- The 0.2 mm depth quantisation is irrelevant at this scale.

Classification: **minor, secondary contributor**, mostly to ICP.

## 13. Root cause assessment

| Category | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed bug | **none** | §2, §3, error metrics (`error_metric_validation.md`), §9, §12 |
| Transform / convention | **excluded** | GT replay 5e-16 m; perfect-correspondence PnP/ICP 5e-6°; controls fail at once |
| Correspondence quality | **primary cause of the systematic drift (both methods)** | feature lag: flow ratio 0.918 mean, 0.72–0.89 at contour/occlusion features; r = +0.70 with PnP's rotation ratio; GT-consistent subset gives 9.94°; same-sign deficits sum to about the final error |
| Algorithmic limitation | **primary cause of ICP's large steps** | no robust loss/rejection in the book ICP; 5.5% gross mismatches; LS optimum has lower RMS than the truth in 35/35 pairs; axis 87° off in 24→25; GT-consistent subset gives 0.71° mean error |
| Depth quality | **minor** | median inconsistency 1.1 mm; 7% > 1 cm, at silhouettes/edges, where it adds to ICP's outliers |
| Inconclusive | **residual interior lag** | even smooth interior features show a ratio of 0.978. A plausible but **untested** cause is aliasing of the sub-pixel triangles (69k triangles on ~14.7k bunny pixels, point-sampled with flat shading), which can make texture appear to move slower than the surface |

## 14. Recommended next investigation

These need the user's approval, because they would change the method or the
data. Nothing here has been done.

1. **Robust ICP, as a measured experiment:** reject 3D pairs by a residual
   threshold, or use RANSAC/Huber around the existing SVD + g2o. Report the
   per-pair error against the current baseline. §8 predicts large gains on
   13→14, 24→25 and 28→29.
2. **Feature-location ablation:** mask ORB detection away from the depth
   silhouette and depth edges, and quantify the change in the bias against
   correspondence count. Use the same metrics as `flow_bias.png`.
3. **Aliasing test:** render a scratch copy of the sequence with supersampling
   (separate dataset, committed data unchanged). Check whether the interior
   flow ratio moves from 0.978 to 1.0.
4. **Sub-pixel depth lookup:** use the depth at the rounded or bilinear
   keypoint location instead of the truncated one. Measure the depth
   consistency of §10 before and after.
5. Only after 1–4: whether a window/pose-graph backend is warranted. It is not
   implemented, and it is not needed to explain the present results.
